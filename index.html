What sets us apart

Spaces built for slowing down.

Alongside a full menu of massage and bodywork, Healing Souls offers spaces built around how nervous systems actually settle down: together in our newly expanded couples room, or in stillness in the salt room and sauna.

The Couples Room

A private, side by side space for two people to receive massage at the same time. Popular for anniversaries, date nights and simply carving out time with someone you care about, without leaving Portage Lakes to do it.

The Salt Room and Sauna

Halotherapy in a dedicated salt-covered space, used by clients managing allergies, sinus congestion, skin conditions and everyday stress. Add our sauna for a longer reset, on its own or alongside any service.

Our approach

Eastern and western care, under one roof.

Ken Morrow founded Healing Souls in 2015 to blend clinical, nervous-system-aware bodywork with traditions like Reiki and energy work, treating them as complementary rather than competing. Every practitioner on staff works from that same starting point: the goal is regulation, not just relaxation.

Healing Souls is also a VA Community Care provider, working directly with physician referrals for veterans in the Akron and Canton area.
