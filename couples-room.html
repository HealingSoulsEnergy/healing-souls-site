Couples Massage · Akron, Ohio

Two chairs, one room, no one watching the clock.

Our private couples room lets two people receive massage side by side, at the same time, in the same quiet space. A favorite for anniversaries, birthdays, or a Tuesday that just needs to feel different.

Book the couples room
The experience

Massage, together, without compromise.

Each of you works with your own practitioner, choosing your own pressure, focus areas and pace, in the same private room at the same time. You're not sharing a single therapist between two appointments; you're sharing a moment.

Our couples room has recently been expanded, giving the two of you more space to settle in.

It works well for couples, close friends, or a parent and adult child looking to spend an hour together doing something other than talking.
